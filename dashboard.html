Difference-in-Differences (DiD) Natural Experiment

Quasi-Experimental Causal Design

Because site and scanner are identical (Philips 3T at University of Amsterdam), the common domain shift between Seen (ID1000) and Unseen (PIOP1+PIOP2) is captured by the supervised scratch baseline. Any excess penalty suffered by the Foundation Model is the true causal effect of pretraining contamination: DiD = (ΔMAE_FM) - (ΔMAE_Baseline).

Pre-Registered Protocol: HYPOTHESIS.md

Formal Hypothesis $H_1$: $\text{DiD} = (\text{MAE}_{\text{FM, Unseen}} - \text{MAE}_{\text{FM, Seen}}) - (\text{MAE}_{\text{Base, Unseen}} - \text{MAE}_{\text{Base, Seen}}) > 0$

Natural Experiment Rationale: Standard domain adaptation benchmarks suffer from confounder leakage: scanner differences, acquisition protocols, demographic variance, and skull-stripping artifacts simultaneously shift when changing cohorts.

By holding scanner (Philips Achieva 3T at University of Amsterdam) and acquisition sequence identical between AOMIC ID1000 (pretraining member) and AOMIC PIOP1/PIOP2 (unseen), the baseline error difference reflects only residual demographic variance. The Foundation Model's excess error difference isolates pretraining memorization from true anatomical generalization.

Pre-registration Criteria: If $\text{DiD} > 0$ with $p < 0.01$ under 10,000 cluster-bootstrap iterations, we reject the null hypothesis of invariant representation transfer.
