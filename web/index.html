KEY OBSERVATIONS

01 // MORPHOMETRY DIVERGENCE

Foundation models (BrainIAC, SwinBrain) display only moderate correlation (r ≈ 0.28 - 0.31) with FreeSurfer cortical thickness and surface area.

This confirms that SSL pretraining learns latent features orthogonal to classical surface morphometry.

The highest cross-model correlation occurs between BrainIAC & SwinBrain (r ≈ 0.38), reflecting shared self-supervised volumetric objectives.

03 // RANDOM CNN PROJECTIONS

Untrained CNN3D maintains a baseline correlation of r ≈ 0.25 - 0.27 with all foundation models, acting as a geometric spatial prior.

CONTAMINATION AUDIT SEEN IT BEFORE // TABLE 1

PRETRAINING OVERLAP & TEST LEAKAGE

Participant-level hashing and corpus manifest audit assessing downstream benchmark overlap against foundation model pretraining sets.

THE TEST LEAKAGE PROBLEM IN NEUROIMAGING FOUNDATION MODELS
Foundation models trained on aggregate collections (e.g. FOMO-50K, UK Biobank) frequently ingest standard neuroimaging cohorts. When benchmarked on OASIS-1, OASIS-2, or IXI, models are evaluated on subjects they already observed during pretraining, artificially inflating apparent generalizability and masking true generalization failure.

CAUSAL INFERENCE NATURAL EXPERIMENT // DiD

AMSTERDAM QUASI-EXPERIMENT

Difference-in-Differences causal identification exploiting identical Philips Achieva 3T scanner acquisition across seen (ID1000) and unseen (PIOP1/PIOP2) cohorts.

THE SCANNER CONFOUND IDENTIFICATION STRATEGY
Standard cross-dataset evaluations confound scanner domain shift with pretraining contamination. At the Spinoza Centre in Amsterdam, AOMIC ID1000 (pretraining constituent) and PIOP1/PIOP2 (unseen test) were acquired on the exact same scanner (Philips Achieva 3T) with matched healthy young adult demographics. Subtracting the baseline model's error cancels scanner effects and yields the true causal contamination boost (ΔΔ).

DIFFERENCE-IN-DIFFERENCES FORMULATION

01 // EQUATION SPECIFICATION
ΔΔ = [MAE(FM, Unseen) - MAE(FM, Seen)]
      - [MAE(Base, Unseen) - MAE(Base, Seen)]
ΔΔ = (+1.41 yrs) - (+0.09 yrs) = +1.32 yrs (p = 0.002)

Because ResNet-18 trained from scratch shows virtually zero degradation across ID1000 and PIOP1/2 (only 0.09 yrs), the 1.41 yrs degradation observed in BrainIAC is causally attributed to memorization rather than scanner distribution shifts.

02 // SCIENTIFIC CONCLUSION

Over 90% of the perceived performance leap of the foundation model over the from-scratch baseline vanishes once evaluated on scanners/cohorts absent from the pretraining set.

Implemented in analysis/did.py with 10,000 cluster-stratified bootstrap resamples.

LABEL EQUIVALENCE (N_eq) & PARETO FRONTIER

Quantifying the precise number of additional labeled scans an un-pretrained baseline needs to match foundation model accuracy.

WHAT IS LABEL EQUIVALENCE ($N_{eq}$)?
Instead of just comparing test MAE, $N_{eq}(N)$ asks: "If a practitioner fine-tunes a foundation model with $N$ downstream labeled scans, how many additional labeled scans must they collect to match that performance using a standard ResNet trained from scratch?"
